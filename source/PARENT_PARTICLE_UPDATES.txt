Parent particle updates (0.28.46)

The converter now handles repeated position/previous-position inheritance for supported parent-child sprite graphs. It keeps independent per-operator selection cursors across simulation passes and preserves authored movement/copy order. Fractional or earlier-time queries do not mutate retained full-tick history.

This uses the converter's approximate 120 Hz clock, birth-order active slots and a deterministic converter random stream. Source buffer compaction, killed-parent dispatch, exact simulation timing and visual parity remain unverified. Scalar copy fields and combinations with locks, model spawning, forces, collisions or other motion remain explicitly unsupported.

Version 0.28.47 adds yaw scalar ramps (field 12). The original Blink smoke graph now bakes all three renderer components and saves/reopens. Independent diagnostic renders confirm output; version 0.28.48 reconstructs the distance-field outline and removes the visible card background in diagnostic renders. Exact Source visual parity is unverified.
