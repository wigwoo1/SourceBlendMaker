Animated CPU trail widths - 0.28.44

Full Oscillate Scalar on width (field 3) now works for render_sprite_trail
with UnlitGeneric materials. No experimental checkbox is required. Positive,
zero and negative animated widths are retained; negative values exchange the
two sides while keeping texture-corner assignments. Rotation (field 4) and
full width oscillation on GPU SpriteCard spline trails remain gated.

UnlitGeneric trails now use the installed CPU corner layout: half the signed
width on each side, perspective eye-relative orientation, current-to-tail
texture order, and the signed radius-to-length constraint (default enabled).
Other material paths retain their previous approximate trail geometry.
